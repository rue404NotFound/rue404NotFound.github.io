// PUBLICATIONS // CV-LISTED WORKS //

An Override-Aware Classifier for Transparent AI

Madzime R.S., Meyer T. & Leenen L.

In Proceedings of the 6th Southern African Conference on AI Research (SACAIR 2025) · December 2025

Introduces the Defeasible Horn Classifier with Exceptions, a symbolic model that pairs default rules with explicit exceptions. The paper shows how Answer Set Programming can learn traceable classifiers that stay competitive while making override reasoning visible.

Toward Defeasible Machine Learning: When Learned Rule Sets Become Defeasible Theories

Madzime R. & Meyer T.

In Proceedings of NMR 2026: 24th International Workshop on Nonmonotonic Reasoning · 2026

Proves that a single structural condition, strict global exception closure, is enough for a learned conjunctive rule set to behave like a defeasible theory. Under that condition, classifier predictions align exactly with Rational Closure, Lexicographic Closure, and System W.

↗ SEE CV FOR DETAILS

Enhanced Electronic Health Record Text Summarization Using Large Language Models

Madzime R. & Nyirenda C.

In Proceedings of the 5th Southern African Conference on AI Research (SACAIR 2024) · December 2024

Fine-tunes Google Flan-T5 to generate clinician-focused summaries of electronic health records from question-answer style prompts. The system emphasizes targeted clinical insight and reported strong Exact Match, ROUGE, and BLEU results.

// RESEARCH THEMES // ANNOTATED INDEX //

THEME-01

Explainable AI

Making machine learning decisions interpretable and auditable for humans — not just optimised for machines.

THEME-02

Defeasible Reasoning

Logic that handles exceptions gracefully. Conclusions hold by default unless something specific overrides them. The KLM framework makes this rigorous.

THEME-03

KLM Framework

Kraus-Lehmann-Magidor nonmonotonic reasoning, including Rational Closure, Lexicographic Closure, and System W — applied to grounding classifier outputs in formal logic.

THEME-04

Interpretable ML

Classifier design that keeps humans in the loop — particularly ECCRS-based symbolic classifiers whose rule structure admits a direct logical reading.

THEME-05

Symbolic & Neuro-Symbolic AI

Bridging the gap between statistical learning and formal symbolic reasoning — exploring where learned representations can be given precise logical semantics.
