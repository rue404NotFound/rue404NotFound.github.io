Research Archive

The formal bridge between symbolic machine learning and defeasible reasoning — when learned classifiers can be read as logical theories. This work connects directly to EU AI Act transparency requirements (Arts. 9, 13, 14): provable, auditable decision logic rather than post-hoc approximations.

// PUBLICATIONS // SELECTED WORKS //

An Override-Aware Classifier for Transparent AI

Madzime R.S., Meyer T. & Leenen L.

In Proceedings of the 6th Southern African Conference on AI Research (SACAIR 2025) · December 2025

This paper introduces the Exception Closed Conjunctive Rule Set (ECCRS) — a class of symbolic classifiers that can be directly read as a defeasible theory. We prove that under the structural condition of strict global exception closure, an ECCRS aligns exactly with Rational Closure, Lexicographic Closure, and System W simultaneously, giving learned rule sets a principled defeasible semantics without post-hoc translation.

Toward Defeasible Machine Learning: When Learned Rule Sets Become Defeasible Theories

Madzime R. & Meyer T.

NMR 2026: 24th International Workshop on Nonmonotonic Reasoning · July 17–19, 2026 · Presenting

A full formal treatment of the conditions under which ECCRS classifiers constitute defeasible theories. We prove that strict global exception closure is the precise structural condition guaranteeing simultaneous alignment with Rational Closure, Lexicographic Closure, and System W — collapsing three distinct defeasible entailment relations onto a single verifiable property of the learned rule set.

Enhanced Electronic Health Record Text Summarization Using Large Language Models

Madzime R. & Nyirenda C.

In Proceedings of the 5th Southern African Conference on AI Research (SACAIR 2024) · December 2024

An investigation into the use of large language models for clinical note summarisation within Electronic Health Record systems. Explainable AI

Making machine learning decisions interpretable and auditable for humans — not just optimised for machines.

THEME-02

Defeasible Reasoning

Logic that handles exceptions gracefully. Conclusions hold "by default" unless specific evidence overrides them — the KLM framework formalises this rigorously.

THEME-03

KLM Framework

Kraus-Lehmann-Magidor nonmonotonic reasoning, including Rational Closure, Lexicographic Closure, and System W — applied to grounding classifier outputs in formal logic.

Interpretable ML

Classifier design that keeps humans in the loop — particularly ECCRS-based symbolic classifiers whose rule structure admits a direct logical reading.

Symbolic & Neuro-Symbolic AI

Bridging the gap between statistical learning and formal symbolic reasoning — exploring where learned representations can be given precise logical semantics.
