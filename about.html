// BIOGRAPHY //

I'm an Explainable AI researcher finishing my MSc at the University of the Western Cape and CAIR. My work tackles a real problem: most AI "explanations" are just educated guesses added after training. I build classifiers that come with logical proofs baked in.

AI explainability without formal guarantees is just a story you tell regulators. My research builds the proof.

My thesis introduces ECCRS classifiers. Each one doubles as a logical theory you can audit step by step: here is the rule that fired, here are the exceptions it checked, here is why it still holds. The goal is to make symbolic classifiers genuinely interpretable instead of merely explainable after the fact. Published at SACAIR 2025 and extended in NMR 2026 work. Supervised by Prof. Louise Leenen and Prof. Thomas Meyer.

I enjoy building research systems that make abstract ideas usable. Right now that includes an ECCRS Explainer App: upload a CSV, generate symbolic rules, and inspect the reasoning visually without needing to code everything by hand.

BSc (Hons) CS with Cum Laude (85%) and BSc CS with Cum Laude (84%), both from UWC. Four Dean's Merit Awards. UWC MasterCard Scholar. Available remotely from Aug 2026.
