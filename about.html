// BIOGRAPHY //

I'm an Explainable AI specialist and published researcher finishing my MSc in Computer Science at the University of the Western Cape and CAIR. My work addresses a concrete problem in enterprise AI: how to build ML classifiers that explain their decisions with the precision that audits and regulation actually require — not post-hoc approximations added after training, but provable logical guarantees built into the model's structure.

AI explainability without formal guarantees is just a story you tell regulators. My research builds the proof.

My MSc thesis introduces ECCRS — Exception-Closed Conjunctive Rule Sets — classifiers I prove are formally defeasible theories under Rational Closure, Lexicographic Closure, and System W simultaneously. Each prediction generates a complete, auditable decision trace: the rule that fired, the exceptions evaluated, and the override logic. This maps directly to the transparency and human oversight requirements in EU AI Act Articles 9, 13, and 14. Published at SACAIR 2025; accepted at NMR 2026 (presenting July) and WiL 2026. Supervised by Prof. Louise Leenen and Prof. Thomas Meyer at CAIR.

I'm transitioning into solutions engineering at AI/ML SaaS companies — specifically those working on XAI, model monitoring, and AI governance. I'm currently building a publicly deployed ECCRS Explainer App in Streamlit: upload a CSV, train a model, generate ECCRS rules, and receive plain-English explanations with SHAP visualisations. Targeting public deployment August 2026. I'm also completing AWS Cloud Practitioner certification and developing SQL fluency for live product demonstrations.

I hold a BSc (Hons) Computer Science with Cum Laude (85%) and a BSc Computer Science with Cum Laude (84%), both from UWC. Four consecutive Dean's Merit Awards and the UWC MasterCard Scholarship. Available for remote SE roles and AI governance consulting from August 2026 — open to companies paying in USD or EUR.
